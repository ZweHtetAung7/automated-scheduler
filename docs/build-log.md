### How the engine works

```
ScheduleInput ──► timeline.build_day() per date ──► DayPlanner.run() per date ──► ScheduleResult
(rows from DB)    Step A: fixed day + capacity      Step B + C: amounts, layout   (schedule_run +
                                                                                   blocks + warnings)
```

`generate_schedule(inputs, start, end, now=None)` is a **pure function**: same inputs, same
output, no database. Days after `end` (up to the latest finish-by date) are still built so
urgency and start-by dates can see the future, but they don't get blocks.

#### Step A: `timeline.py` (one day)

1. **Awake window** = wake time to bedtime for that weekday (H1, H2), or the one-day override.
   A bedtime at or before the wake time counts as after midnight. **H3 (min sleep):** if the
   weekly bedtime leaves less than 8h before tomorrow's wake, bedtime moves earlier and a note is
   added. A one-day override the user typed wins, but they get a note too.
2. **Events** that day: weekly ones on matching weekdays, one-off ones by date (trips can span
   several days). `event_exceptions` cancel or move single dates (A4, the coach moved practice).
3. **Pre-game** (H7): games occupy 2h before kickoff (`pre_game` block).
4. **Travel** (H13): starting from home, a 15 min travel block goes before any event at a
   different location, plus one home after the last event. None inside an away trip.
5. **Meals** (H4): breakfast 15 min after waking, lunch 12:00, dinner 6:30. They shift up to
   ±30 min to dodge events. If that fails, they move to the nearest gap within 3h. Dinner tries
   *after* first ("moves after practice"), so a game day gets a post-game meal. Breakfast stays
   within an hour of waking.
6. **Zones** (no activity of their own, just rules):
   - `recovery` (H12): 2h after games, 1h after hard practices. No study; meals and travel OK.
   - `trip` (H9): away travel with `study_during` = none (no study) or light (light tasks only).
   - `pregame` (S2): before noon on game day, and from 8pm the night before.
   - `low_energy` (S9): 2h after practice or a game (after recovery), and on light-study trips.
7. **Free intervals** = awake window minus events, meals, travel, recovery and no-study trips.
   **H14 break rule:** a free interval starts 10 min after a meal or event and ends 10 min
   before one. No break is needed next to travel, recovery, waking or bedtime.
8. **Capacity:** load factor (class 1, practice 1.5, game 2.5... × intensity), fatigue penalty
   = 5% per load unit (max 50%). `usable focus = free time × (1 − penalty)`. Daily max = 5h ×
   season multiplier (S6), × 0.6 on game/travel days (S5: 5h → 3h). **Study cap** = the smaller
   of the two.

#### Step B: `priority.py` (per task, per day)

- `remaining` = estimate × 1.25 (A1) × (1 − % complete), rounded up to 5 min.
- `finish_by` = deadline − 12h (H20).
- `task_cap(day)` = the most this task could get that day on its own: min(2h, day's cap, free
  time it's allowed to use before its finish-by and after its release).
- **Priority** = the formula in constraints-v1.md section 4, unchanged (R1-R8 weights from
  settings). Scaling: grade weight full at 25%, remaining work full at 10h, start-by pressure 0
  when start-by is 7+ days away. Tie-breakers: earlier deadline, higher grade, already started.
- **Planning days** = today up to the day *before* the finish-by day. The scheduler aims to be
  done a day early; the finish-by day itself is only a fallback.
- **Start-by (A2)** = the latest planning day the task can start and still fit at 2h/day.
  `None` means it doesn't fit even starting today.
- **Mandatory today** = remaining − everything later days could still hold. If > 0, skipping
  today misses the deadline.
- **Allotment** = 1.5h (S1), raised to 2h (H15) when 1.5h a day over the planning days isn't
  enough. This rule produces the essay's 2/2/2/1.5h in Scenario 3.

#### Step C: `placement.py` (per day)

1. **Amounts** (how much of each task today, total ≤ study cap):
   - pass 1: mandatory minutes, earliest finish-by first;
   - pass 2: top up each task to its allotment, highest priority first;
   - tidy: no amount under 30 min unless it finishes the task.
2. **Layout** (where): tasks that can't wait go first, then by priority. Each block goes in the
   lowest-cost slot. Cost = hours after midnight (earlier is better), plus soft-constraint
   adjustments in hours:

   | Rule | Cost |
   |---|---|
   | Heavy task (difficulty 4-5) in peak hours (S4) | −4 |
   | Light task (difficulty 1-2) in peak hours | +2 (keep peak for hard work) |
   | Light task in a low-energy slot (S9 / your post-game answer) | −8 |
   | Heavy task in a low-energy slot | +6 |
   | Heavy task in the pre-game window (S2) | +50 |
   | 3rd different course in one evening (S10) | +20 |
   | Block shorter than it could be (task gets split) | +6 |

   Blocks are 30-90 min (H17, H18), never leave a scrap under 30 min (the 65-minute example in
   test day 1), and stay 10 min from other study blocks (H14).
3. **Dependencies (H22):** a task waits until its prerequisites are fully placed. If one
   finishes today, the dependent can start after it the same day.
4. **Explanations:** every study block stores `reasons` (a machine code + a sentence), e.g.
   `raised_to_max`: "2h today, above your 1.5h preference, because 7.5h remain and 4 day(s) at
   your preferred pace wouldn't finish it a day early."

#### Warnings: `engine.py`

| Kind | When |
|---|---|
| `shortfall` (A3, critical) | A task due within the plan can't be fully placed. Says how many hours short and lists trade-offs: raise the daily max on the days that hit it, allow normal study during travel, a smaller finish buffer, ask for an extension |
| `start_by` (A2) | Not started and start-by is today (info), or it won't finish a day early even starting now (warning) |
| `start_by_moved` (A4) | `start_by_changes(old, new)` compares two runs, e.g. before and after adding a trip |
| `overdue` | The finish-by time has already passed |
| `blocked` | Waiting on a prerequisite |
| `day_note` | Bedtime moved, a meal couldn't fit, tight travel |
| Rule notices | First game / trip / hard practice / work shift. Shown only if not in `seen_rule_notices` (your `rule_activations.notice_shown`) |

### Decisions I made that you should review

1. **Local time inside the engine.** The scheduler uses naive local datetimes. Converting to and
   from UTC (how `data-model.md` stores times) will be the API layer's job, which keeps the
   engine and its tests free of time zones.
2. **The H14 break rule** (see Step A.7). Test day 1 has no break between breakfast and the
   essay but does have one after lunch. I picked one consistent rule, so the essay starts at
   9:25 instead of 9:15.
3. **Aim to finish a day early** (planning days). This is what makes the essay front-load
   (2, 2, 2, 1.5h, Friday free), as your table expects.
4. **Must-do-today work goes first** (earliest finish-by), before priority. Without this, in test
   day 2 the essay (highest priority) would take the slot MATH needs, and MATH would miss
   tonight's 9pm finish-by.
5. **Your post-game answer (soft rule).** In test day 2 this *swaps* two tasks compared with the
   doc's table: the light ENG reading goes after the game (8:00pm) and the heavy essay before it
   (12:55pm). All six checks still hold. A second test shows heavy work still lands after a game
   when nothing light is left.
6. **"Light" = difficulty 1-2, "heavy" = 4-5** (`energy.light_max_difficulty`,
   `pregame.heavy_min_difficulty`).
7. **Soft-constraint costs** are constants at the top of `placement.py`, not user settings yet.
   They need tuning on real weeks before they're worth exposing.
8. **Added the `pre_game` block kind** (the 2h arrival before a game). `data-model.md`'s
   `scheduled_blocks.kind` enum doesn't have it yet.

### Known limitations

- **Greedy, not optimal.** Some days get choppy blocks (e.g. Thursday in test day 3: 35 + 55 min
  instead of one 90 min block). That's the expected trade-off of greedy; OR-Tools CP-SAT
  (roadmap Phase 5) is the fix if it bothers you in real use.
- Start-by and "mandatory" look at each task alone, ignoring competition for the same hours.
  Two big tasks sharing a deadline can each look fine alone and still collide. The shortfall
  warning, computed after placement, is what catches that.
- Daylight-saving nights: durations across the 1-hour shift are off by an hour. Only sleep
  spans those, so no study block is affected.
